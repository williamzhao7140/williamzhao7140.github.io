Subscription Help

Milkbun subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID and the App Store.

To manage or cancel your subscription:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your Apple ID
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select Milkbun
  5. Manage or cancel your subscription

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request refunds through Apple's support.
